e-Passport Fees and Payment Options - Sri Lanka

Last Update: 21 January 2025

e-Passport Payment Methods

  • Online Payment: Available through the official portal.
  • Offline Payment: Payments can be made at any government or private bank through an A-Challan.

e-Passport online Payment Methods & Guidelines

  • Bank Transfer: Payments can be made via bank transfer to the designated government account.
    Guidelines:
    • Visit your bank or use online banking.
    • Transfer the exact amount to the official government account.
    • Save the transaction receipt and note the transaction ID.
    • Upload the receipt while submitting the application.
  • Online Payment: Secure transactions can be made through the official portal.
    Guidelines:
    • Log in to the e-Passport application portal.
    • Select "Online Payment" as your payment method.
    • Follow the instructions to complete payment via a secure gateway.
    • Save the payment confirmation receipt.
  • Credit/Debit Card: Payments can be made instantly using Visa/MasterCard.
    Guidelines:
    • Ensure your card is activated for online transactions.
    • Select "Credit/Debit Card" as your payment method.
    • Enter card details and complete authentication.
    • Download and upload the transaction receipt.
  • Mobile Payment: Payments can be made via mobile banking apps or digital wallets.
    Guidelines:
    • Use a supported mobile banking or wallet service.
    • Select "Mobile Payment" as your payment method.
    • Scan the provided QR code or enter payment details.
    • Save the payment confirmation for future reference.
  • Offline Payment: Payments can be made at any government or private bank through an A-Challan.
    Guidelines:
    • Download and print the A-Challan from the official website.
    • Visit the nearest authorized bank.
    • Make the payment at the counter.
    • Collect the stamped receipt and upload it during application submission.
  • Other Payment Methods: Additional options may be available based on service updates.
    Guidelines:
    • Check the official portal for newly added payment options.
    • Follow the specified steps for the chosen method.
    • Ensure you retain proof of payment.

e-Passport Offline Payment Methods & Guidelines


e-Passport Processing Time

  • Regular Delivery: 15 Working days / 21 days from the biometric enrolment date.
  • Express Delivery: 7 Working days / 10 days from the biometric enrolment date.
  • Super Express Delivery: 2 Working days from the biometric enrolment date.
  • Government employees: Those with NOC/Retired documentation (PRL) will get express facilities at regular fees.

Eligibility for e-Passport

All Sri Lankan citizens are eligible to apply for an e-Passport. However, applicants must have valid identification and supporting documents.

Documents Required for e-Passport

  • National Identity Card (NIC) or Birth Certificate
  • Previous Passport (if applicable)
  • Biometric Enrollment Receipt
  • Police Report (if applying for lost passport replacement)
  • Marriage Certificate (if applicable for name change)
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) for government employees

How to Apply for an e-Passport?

To apply for an e-Passport, follow these steps:

  1. Visit the Department of Immigration and Emigration website to download the application form.
  2. Fill in the required details accurately.
  3. Collect all necessary supporting documents.
  4. Pay the applicable fee via A-Challan at a government or private bank.
  5. Submit your application and complete the biometric enrollment process.
  6. Collect your passport from the assigned passport office after the specified processing time.

Important Notes

  • Ensure all documents are valid and accurate before submission.
  • Biometric enrollment is mandatory for all applicants.
  • Super Express service is only available for applicants with previous passports.
  • Fees are subject to change based on government policies.
