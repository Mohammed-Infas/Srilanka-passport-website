Urgent Applicaitons

Last update: 24 January 2025

The Super Express Passport Service is designed for Sri Lankan citizens who require a new passport urgently. This service ensures the fastest possible processing and issuance of an e-Passport within two working days under specific conditions and applicable fees. Below is a detailed explanation of the process, eligibility, and key details about obtaining an urgent passport in Sri Lanka.


What is the Super Express Passport Service?

In certain situations, individuals may need a passport on an urgent basis due to emergencies such as:

  • Last-minute travel for business or work commitments.
  • Medical emergencies requiring travel.
  • Unexpected family emergencies abroad.
  • Urgent visa applications.

    • To cater to these needs, the Super Express Delivery option is available for eligible applicants. This expedited process guarantees that the passport will be ready within two (2) working days after the application is successfully submitted and verified.

    Who can apply for super express delivery?

    The Super Express Passport Service is available to all Sri Lankan citizens who need urgent passport issuance. However, certain conditions apply:

  • This service is primarily for applicants who already have a previous passport.
  • If an applicant is applying for their first passport or has changed their permanent address, additional verification may be required, which may delay processing.
  • The service is available only to those who apply at designated passport offices in Sri Lanka.

    • Where can I apply for super express passport?

    Applications for the Super Express Passport Service can be submitted at any domestic passport office in Sri Lanka.

  • This service is NOT available at Sri Lankan embassies or consulates abroad.
  • Applicants must visit a local passport office in person to complete biometric enrolment and application submission.

    • Unlike standard passport applications, which may be delivered to various passport offices, the Super Express Passport is only delivered to a specific location:
    📍 Divisional Passport and Visa Office, Agargaon, Dhaka-1207
    🏛 Building-02, E-7, Sher-E-Bangla Nagar, Agargaon, Dhaka-1207
    📞 Contact No: +880 2-8123788

  • Applicants MUST collect their passport from this designated office.
  • Shipment to other passport offices is NOT possible under this service.
  • If an applicant is unable to collect the passport in person, an authorized representative may be allowed to collect it on their behalf (with proper identification and authorization documents).
