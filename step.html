5 Steps to your e-Passport

Last update: 21 Janvary 2025

You can apply for the new e-Passport in 5 easy steps.

Step 1: Check if the new e-Passport is available in your area

  • Before applying, it is important to confirm whether the new e-Passport service is available in your region. The Sri Lankan Department of Immigration and Emigration has designated several offices across the country to facilitate e-Passport issuance.
    1. Applicants should refer to the List of Functional e-Passport Offices to find the nearest service center.
    2. If your area is not yet covered, you may need to visit a nearby city where e-Passport services are available. Here

    • Step 2: Fill in your e-Passport application online

  • To streamline the application process, applicants can complete the e-Passport application form online before visiting a passport office.
  • The online form requires details such as personal information, identification documents, and previous passport details (if applicable).
  • Double-check all information before submission to avoid errors or delays.
  • Once submitted, you may receive a reference number or appointment confirmation for your biometric enrolment. Here

    • Step 3: Pay passport fees

  • Passport applications require a processing fee, which must be paid at authorized banks or through the designated online payment system.
  • The fees may vary based on the type of passport (regular, urgent processing) and the applicant’s category (adult, minor, renewal, lost passport, etc.).
  • Payments should be made only at approved banks listed by the Department of Immigration and Emigration to ensure security and validity.
  • Applicants should keep the payment receipt, as it will be required during the biometric enrolment process. Here

    • Step 4: Visit your Passport Office for biometric enrolment

  • Once you have successfully submitted your application and paid the passport fee, you must visit your nearest passport office for biometric enrolment.
    Biometric enrolment includes fingerprint scanning, facial recognition, and digital signature capture.
    Ensure you bring all required documents, including:
    1. National Identity Card (NIC)
    2. Previous passport (if applicable)
    3. Payment receipt
    4. Any additional supporting documents (birth certificate, marriage certificate, etc.)
    It is advisable to arrive early to avoid long queues and ensure a smooth enrolment process.

    • Step 5: Collect your e-Passport at the passport office

  • Once your e-Passport is processed and ready, you can collect it from the passport office.
    During your biometric enrolment, you will receive a delivery slip, which you must present when collecting your passport.
    If you are unable to collect it in person, an authorized representative can collect the passport on your behalf. The representative must bring: Their National Identity Card (NIC) The applicant’s original delivery slip
    Ensure that all details in the new passport are correct before leaving the passport office.
