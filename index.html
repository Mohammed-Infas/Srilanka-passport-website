Important Notices

View All Notices
  • Steps to Apply for an e-Passport Online: Visit the official website and create an account to start your application process. Fill in the necessary personal details and upload the required documents.
  • Required Documents for e-Passport Enrollment: You will need your National Identity Card, Birth Certificate, proof of address, and a recent passport-sized photo to complete your application.
  • How to Track Your e-Passport Application Status: Once your application is submitted, use the application reference number to track the status on the official portal.
  • Temporary Closure of Passport Services Due to Covid-19: Passport services will be temporarily halted during the Covid-19 lockdown. Please stay updated through official channels for re-opening notifications.
  • Notice Regarding Delays in Passport Processing: Due to high demand, there may be delays in processing e-passports. Applicants are advised to apply well in advance to avoid inconvenience.
